# Acme Inc. — Employee & Customer Handbook

_Last updated: 2026. This document is sample content for demonstrating
retrieval-augmented generation. All policies are fictional._

## 1. Working Hours & Remote Work

Acme operates on a remote-first basis. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to
3:00 PM in each employee's local time zone, during which team members should be
reachable for meetings and synchronous discussion. Outside of core hours,
employees are free to structure their day as they see fit, provided deliverables
and on-call commitments are met.

Employees may work from any location, but those handling regulated customer data
must do so from a private, secured network — never public Wi-Fi without the
company VPN enabled. Anyone relocating to a new country for more than 30 days
must notify People Ops in advance, as it can affect tax and compliance status.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO)

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, in addition to 10
company holidays. PTO accrues monthly and unused days roll over up to a maximum
of 30 days. Requests should be submitted at least two weeks in advance for
absences longer than three days. There is no cap on sick leave, but absences of
more than five consecutive days may require a doctor's note.

New parents are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which may be
taken any time within the first year following birth or adoption.

## 3. Customer Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no questions
asked. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business
days. After 30 days, refunds are offered as store credit only.

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the account dashboard.
Cancellation stops future billing but does not retroactively refund the current
billing period. For annual plans cancelled within the first 14 days, a full
refund is issued; after 14 days, a prorated refund of unused months is provided.

For enterprise contracts, refund terms are defined in the signed agreement and
override this general policy.

## 4. Security & Data Handling

All company laptops must have full-disk encryption enabled and screen lock set to
no more than five minutes of inactivity. Production credentials are never stored
in source control; they are managed exclusively through the company secrets
manager. Access to customer data follows the principle of least privilege and is
reviewed quarterly.

Any suspected security incident — a lost device, a phishing click, an exposed
key — must be reported to the security team within one hour of discovery.
Reporting promptly is always treated as the right call, even if it turns out to
be a false alarm. Employees are never penalized for reporting in good faith.

Two-factor authentication is mandatory on all company accounts. Passwords must be
unique per service and stored in the approved password manager.

## 5. Expense Reimbursement

Reasonable business expenses are reimbursed within two weeks of submission.
Receipts are required for any expense over $25. Each employee has a $1,000 annual
home-office budget for equipment, and a $1,500 annual learning budget for
courses, books, and conferences. Travel for company offsites is fully covered,
including economy airfare, accommodation, and meals up to $75 per day.

Expenses must be submitted within 60 days of being incurred; submissions after
that window require manager approval.

## 6. Code of Conduct

Acme is committed to a respectful, inclusive workplace. Harassment,
discrimination, and retaliation are not tolerated in any form. Disagreements
should be handled directly and professionally; when that is not possible, People
Ops is available to mediate confidentially.

Conflicts of interest — financial or personal — must be disclosed proactively.
Employees may not accept gifts over $100 in value from vendors or customers.

## 7. Customer Support SLAs

Support tickets are triaged by severity. Critical issues affecting production for
enterprise customers receive a first response within one hour, 24/7. High-priority
issues are answered within four business hours. Standard requests receive a
response within one business day. Resolution times depend on complexity, but
customers receive a status update at least once per business day until the issue
is closed.

Customers on the Enterprise plan have access to a dedicated support engineer and
a private Slack channel. Free-tier users receive community and email support only.
