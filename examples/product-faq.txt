Acme Cloud — Frequently Asked Questions
(Sample content for RAG demonstration. Fictional product.)

PLANS & PRICING

Q: What plans does Acme Cloud offer?
A: There are three plans. Starter is free and includes 1 project, 1 GB of
storage, and community support. Pro is $29 per user per month and adds unlimited
projects, 100 GB of storage, priority email support, and the full API. Enterprise
is custom-priced and adds SSO, a dedicated support engineer, a 99.9% uptime SLA,
and on-premise deployment options.

Q: Can I change plans at any time?
A: Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the current billing
period. Downgrades take effect at the start of the next billing cycle so you keep
paid features until then.

Q: Do you offer discounts?
A: Annual billing saves two months compared to monthly. We also offer 50% off for
registered nonprofits and free Pro access for accredited students.

BILLING & CANCELLATION

Q: How do I cancel?
A: Cancel any time from Settings > Billing. Cancellation stops future charges; the
current period is not refunded except where local law requires otherwise.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: All major credit cards and, on annual Enterprise plans, bank transfer (ACH and
SEPA). Invoicing is available for Enterprise customers.

DATA & SECURITY

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: Data is stored in SOC 2 Type II certified data centers. You can choose a US or
EU region at project creation. The region cannot be changed after creation.

Q: Can I export my data?
A: Yes. A full export (JSON or CSV) is available from the dashboard at any time,
on every plan including the free tier. There is no lock-in.

Q: Is my data encrypted?
A: Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256). Customer-managed
encryption keys are available on the Enterprise plan.

API & LIMITS

Q: Are there API rate limits?
A: The free plan allows 60 requests per minute. Pro allows 600 per minute.
Enterprise limits are negotiated per contract. Exceeding the limit returns HTTP
429; back off and retry with exponential delay.

Q: Do you have webhooks?
A: Yes. Webhooks are available on Pro and Enterprise and can be configured per
project. Failed deliveries are retried for up to 24 hours.

SUPPORT

Q: How do I reach support?
A: Free users get community and email support. Pro users get priority email with a
four-business-hour first response. Enterprise users get 24/7 support and a private
Slack channel with a dedicated engineer.
